Scaling Your Funded Account

Scaling your funded account is the ultimate goal at Yung FX Capital Prop Firm. We provide you with the capital, but it's your skills and habits that will take your account to the next level.

Whether you're new to prop trading or an experienced trader, scaling is a process — and we're here to guide you through each stage. Learn how to increase your capital while keeping your risk in check.

📌 Prove Consistency Before Scaling

The first step in scaling your account is proving consistency. You’ll need to demonstrate that you can handle the capital responsibly. We look for traders who have a solid track record of sticking to their trading plan, managing risk, and following our rules.

At Yung FX Capital, we track your performance, and once you show consistency over a set period, scaling opportunities open up for you.

⚖️ Stay Within Risk Limits

Scaling isn’t about taking bigger risks — it’s about maintaining discipline while managing larger amounts of capital. Our funding models are designed to protect you, so you can focus on steady, controlled growth.

We help traders expand their limits gradually while ensuring they never exceed risk thresholds. You'll learn how to manage drawdown, profit-taking, and trade sizes for optimal scaling.

🔄 Understand the Scaling Process

As you prove yourself with smaller accounts, you’ll be eligible to scale up to larger accounts. Our scaling process is simple and transparent:

  • Start with a funded account at a specific size
  • Follow risk management rules and hit performance targets
  • Request scaling after demonstrating consistent profitability
  • We evaluate your performance and adjust your account size

This progression allows you to manage more capital responsibly, without overwhelming you at any point.

⏳ Don’t Rush the Process

Scaling takes time. You won’t go from $10K to $100K overnight. The key is to focus on improving your trading skills, managing your psychology, and adhering to the rules.

We’ll be with you every step of the way, providing feedback and advice to ensure you’re on the right track. Growth is a marathon, not a sprint.

💰 Know When to Take Profits

Part of scaling involves taking profits along the way. You don’t need to risk everything on each trade. Profit-taking is a crucial habit that allows you to secure your gains and manage the growth of your account responsibly.

At Yung FX Capital, we emphasize profit-taking strategies that help you scale gradually while minimizing the risk of significant drawdowns.

✅ Next Steps

Scaling your funded account is about consistency, patience, and discipline. Focus on long-term growth, and we’ll help you unlock higher account sizes and greater opportunities.
