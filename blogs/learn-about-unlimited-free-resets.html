Learn About Unlimited Free Resets

At Yung FX Capital Prop Firm, we understand that growth comes with setbacks. That’s why we offer something most firms don’t: Unlimited Free Resets.

Whether you violated drawdown or had a rough trading week — we believe in second chances, and third, and fourth… because the journey to consistency takes time.

📌 What Is a Reset?

A reset wipes your trading account clean — your balance, history, and trades are cleared, and you start fresh with a new funded account under the same conditions.

It’s not a penalty. It’s an opportunity to bounce back stronger and smarter.

🔄 How Our Unlimited Resets Work

If you breach your account rules (e.g. daily or total drawdown), you’ll enter a 12-day cooldown period. After that, you qualify for a free reset — no hidden fees, no extra costs.

You can do this as many times as needed. Your journey doesn't end after one mistake.

💡 Why We Offer This

Most firms eliminate you after one failure. We don’t believe in that. At Yung FX Capital, we back trader development, not perfection. We’d rather help you grow into a six-figure trader than cut you off early.

Reset access builds confidence, encourages discipline, and reduces fear of failure — which leads to better trading decisions.

👥 Who Qualifies for Resets?

Any trader who has an active funded account with us and respects our reset policy. After the 12-day reset window, you're eligible — no application required.

Traders abusing the system intentionally will be reviewed and may face restrictions. This system is built for honest growth, not shortcuts.

⚡ How to Request a Reset

After 12 days from your breach, visit your dashboard and click the “Request Reset” button. Our system will automatically issue your new account within minutes.

No support tickets. No waiting days. You’re back on track instantly — because momentum matters.

✅ Next Steps

Unlimited resets mean unlimited chances to grow. Stay disciplined, keep improving, and use resets as a tool to reach consistency, not as a crutch.
