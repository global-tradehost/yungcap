If you're serious about trading and ready to turn your skill into income, here’s exactly how to get funded with us — step by step.

1. Choose Your Funding Model

We offer multiple instant funding models — Starter, Standard, Rapid, Growth, and Custom — to match every trader's style and experience level.

Select the one that aligns with your goals, account size preference, and risk appetite. Each model has clear rules and payout systems.

2. Register & Activate Your Account

Once you've selected your model, sign up on our platform and activate your account. You’ll instantly receive your funded trading credentials via email or dashboard.

No evaluations. No delays. You're trading with live capital from day one.

3. Follow the Trading Rules

To stay funded and unlock higher payouts, you must respect our drawdown rules, risk limits, and trading guidelines. They’re designed to protect both your growth and our capital.

Break the rules? No problem — we offer unlimited free resets after a 12-day reset window. You’ll never be punished for failing — only for quitting.

4. Withdraw Your Profits

Hit your profit targets? Withdraw anytime based on your model’s payout cycle. We pay fast, securely, and with transparency. No hidden fees. No delays.

Your skill earns. Your results speak. And your payout is the proof.

5. Scale Your Account Over Time

Consistent traders unlock bigger accounts. As you follow the rules and build profits, we’ll scale your account size — up to $200K and beyond — through our performance-based milestones.

We don’t just fund traders. We build capital managers.
